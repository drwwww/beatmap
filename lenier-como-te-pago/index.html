Lenier — Como Te Pago

(How Do I Repay You) 2020 salsabachata 🇨🇺 CU

Trending since May 22, 2026 · peaked at 14 charts

Ay, ojalá que nunca se apague la luz que tienen tus ojos

Cuban reggaetón singer Lenier wrote Como Te Pago as a mother's-day ballad in reggaetón clothing — its hook asks, literally, how you repay someone for nine months carried in the womb — and that subject matter has given it a perennial calendar pulse that most tracks in the genre don't have. Released in May 2020, the song returns to broad chart presence each spring across Latin American and U.S. Hispanic-market platforms, tracking closely with the lead-up to Día de las Madres celebrations. Lenier built his following largely through the independent Miami-Cuban circuit before crossover streaming exposure widened his reach; Como Te Pago remains his most-recognized title. It is currently tracking across 14 country and platform charts, back after an 11-day gap — a pattern consistent with a track that resurfaces on sentiment rather than industry push.
