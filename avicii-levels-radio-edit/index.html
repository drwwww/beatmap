A cornerstone of early-2010s festival dance is back on the global charts today, tracked across 56 country and platform charts after a six-week absence. Built around a chopped Etta James sample and one of the most recognizable synth hooks of the decade, the track — which premiered untitled on Pete Tong's Essential Mix on BBC Radio 1 in December 2010, per Wikipedia — helped push electronic music into Top 40 radio, a shift several outlets credit to Tim Bergling directly, according to Tunefind. It earned him a 2013 Grammy nomination. The song remains a fixture of Avicii tribute edits circulating on TikTok, where fans continue to memorialize the producer, who died in 2018. Its broad return today underlines how durably the record has held its place in the dance canon.
