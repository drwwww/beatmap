Jennifer Hudson's Pharrell-produced neo-soul single from her third studio album is broadly charting today across 136 country and platform charts, returning after a 16-day gap. The track landed with considerable pedigree on arrival — Pharrell Williams production wrapped around Hudson's full-register delivery, with a T.I. verse giving it crossover traction — but it never fully broke through commercially the way her earlier work had. Worth knowing: per Wikipedia, the song was originally conceived for Miley Cyrus's Bangerz sessions, with Tyler, the Creator slated for the rap verse, before the track was redirected entirely. That 70s-soul construction — warm bass, call-and-response hook, deliberately unhurried tempo — has given it a longer shelf life than the mid-2010s R&B moment it arrived in. Hudson has remained publicly visible through American Idol and her talk show, which keeps her catalog accessible to new audiences discovering backward.
