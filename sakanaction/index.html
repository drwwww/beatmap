The Sapporo-formed art-rock band's sleek 2013 single is broadly charting today across 29 country and platform charts, returning after a brief gap. Originally written as a commercial song for the fashion school Mode Gakuen — featuring choreographer Jonte Morning — "夜の踊り子" stretches well beyond its ad-brief origins: at five minutes, it layers motorik rhythm with sakanaction's signature blend of post-punk guitar and electronic sheen, arriving at something closer to dance-floor minimalism than product placement. The track has accumulated over 26 million Spotify streams and remains one of the band's most-streamed catalog cuts behind "Kaiju." No specific event appears to have triggered this particular resurgence, but the song's architectural restraint — a single propulsive groove held across its full runtime — has made it a durable entry point for listeners discovering the band's back catalog.
