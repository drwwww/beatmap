2Pac — Dear Mama

1995 old school hip hopwest coast hip hopgangster rap 🇺🇸 US

Trending since May 26, 2026 · peaked at 144 charts

You are appreciated

The five-part Paramount+ documentary series Dear Mama — which traces the intertwined lives of Tupac Shakur and his mother, Black Panther activist Afeni Shakur — has kept this 1995 single in sustained global circulation since its 2022–2023 run, and the song is broadly charting today across 144 country and platform charts. The track was also featured in the 2003 documentary Tupac: Resurrection, cementing its role as the anchor of Shakur's biographical record on screen. Originally the lead single from Me Against the World — which, per Wikipedia, debuted at number one on the Billboard 200 while Shakur was incarcerated — the song samples Joe Sample's piano and The Spinners to build a West Coast soul backdrop for one of rap's most direct maternal tributes. Few tracks from the mid-nineties have remained this structurally durable across Mother's Day season and anniversary cycles alike.
