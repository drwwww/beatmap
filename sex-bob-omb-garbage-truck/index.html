The fictional garage band from Scott Pilgrim vs. The World is broadly charting today, tracked across 133 country and platform charts. Garbage Truck was one of four Sex Bob-Omb songs written by Beck for the 2010 Edgar Wright film, all performed by the cast — the track soundtracks Scott's first encounter with one of Ramona's evil exes, per Genius. A full-length promo video of the band's Battle of the Bands performance, uninterrupted by the on-screen fight, was produced for the DVD release, according to the Scott Pilgrim Wiki. It remains a durable cut of lo-fi garage rock — two minutes of fuzzed-out riffing and a deadpan hook about hauling someone to the dump.
