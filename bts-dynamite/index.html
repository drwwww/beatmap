BTS — Dynamite

2020 k-pop 🇰🇷 KR

Trending since July 21, 2026 · peaked at 328 charts

'Cause I, I, I'm in the stars tonight
So, watch me bring the fire, and set the night alight

BTS performed Dynamite at the inaugural FIFA World Cup halftime show on July 19, appearing on a central stage at MetLife Stadium during a slot introduced for the first time in the tournament's 96-year history, per The Hollywood Reporter. The 11-minute set adapted the band's English-language single before a global broadcast audience — and it is now appearing on 328 country and platform charts. According to STARNEWS, Jungkook's presence extends a record of performing on multiple World Cup stages, following the 2022 Qatar opening. The disco-pop track, written by David Stewart and Jessica Agombar, has drawn 2.3 billion Spotify streams since 2020, notes Sporting News. A televised event of this scale reliably resets a catalog anthem's reach; the breadth here reflects it.
