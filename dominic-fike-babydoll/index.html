"Babydoll" is sitting at number one on Spotify globally — seven years after Fike quietly dropped it on his debut EP, Don't Forget About Me, Demos, while he was reportedly still under house arrest in Florida. According to MuseScore, the track has seen a 175% increase in streams during its early-2026 resurgence, driven by a wave of TikTok edits — breakup montages, longing-core slideshows, lyric-breakdown videos — that found new audiences for its compact, minor-key ache. A re-release on Spotify alongside a companion track, "White Keys," gave both recordings a structural streaming boost. The AOL report also notes Fike's return to Euphoria landing in the same window, keeping his name in the cultural conversation. Now tracking across 64 country and platform charts, "Babydoll" is among the more striking catalog revivals of the year — a bedroom-recorded lo-fi sketch that has somehow outlasted the album cycle, the acting arc, and the press cycle that followed.
