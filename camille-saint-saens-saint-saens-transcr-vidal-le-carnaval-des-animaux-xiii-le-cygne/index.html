Camille Saint-Saëns, Gautier Capuçon, Douglas Boyd, Orchestre de chambre de Paris — Saint-Saëns / Transcr. Vidal: Le carnaval des animaux: XIII. Le cygne

(The Swan) 2017 classical 🇫🇷 FR

Trending since July 2, 2026 · peaked at 35 charts

Cellist Gautier Capuçon's reading of Saint-Saëns' most famous miniature is broadly charting today, tracked across 35 country and platform charts after a 20-day absence. Written in 1886 as the thirteenth movement of Le carnaval des animaux — a suite Saint-Saëns kept from public performance during his lifetime, per Wikipedia — "Le cygne" is the one section he permitted, and it survives as a standard of the cello repertoire. The piece has a long screen life: IMDb lists it among Saint-Saëns' most-synced compositions across decades of film. Capuçon's 2017 recording, later folded into playlists like Classical Chillout, sits squarely in the streaming-era focus-and-calm ecosystem that keeps instrumental catalog reliably in rotation. Its return is the kind of quiet re-entry these ambient-classical picks tend to make.
