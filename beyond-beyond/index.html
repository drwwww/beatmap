A 1991 Cantorock anthem by Hong Kong's most enduring band is broadly charting today, appearing on 76 country and platform charts after a week-long gap. 不再猶豫 — a song about pushing through self-doubt toward an ideal — sits among the defining works of Beyond, the quartet whose frontman Wong Ka Kui died in 1993 and whose surviving members, per Wikipedia, reconvened that November for a Composer's Tribute Night. The track's anthemic build and clenched-fist refrain have made it a perennial fixture at Cantonese-language graduations, protests, and karaoke nights across the diaspora. It remains one of the band's most-streamed catalog cuts, a piece of Cantopop's defiant streak that travels easily across generations. The presence stretches widely today across East and Southeast Asian charts.
