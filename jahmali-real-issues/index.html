A late-90s Penthouse Records cut from Jamaican cultural singer Jahmali is broadly charting today, tracked across 132 country and platform charts. Real Issues is conscious roots reggae in the Penthouse house style — a label that built its reputation on dancehall in the early 90s before pivoting toward the rootsier, message-driven material that Jahmali specialized in. The track's appeal is in its plainspoken protest: a meditation on global power and "new world order" disorder, delivered over a measured one-drop riddim. Reissued digitally in 2019 as part of the Penthouse Flashback catalog push (YouTube), it now circulates on streaming alongside the label's deep archive. Its broad presence across so many charts at once marks one of the wider reggae-catalog footprints visible today.
