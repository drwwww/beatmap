Future, Rihanna — Selfish (feat. Rihanna)

2017 rap 🇺🇸 US

Trending since June 17, 2026 · peaked at 46 charts

A deep cut from Future's self-titled era is broadly charting today, tracked across 46 country and platform charts after a 24-day absence. Pulled from the rapper's 2017 album FUTURE, Selfish pairs his Auto-Tuned melancholy with one of Rihanna's only guest turns of that year — a feature that has accrued roughly 271 million Spotify streams in the years since, per mystreamcount. The track sits at the softer end of Future's catalog, a slow-burn duet rather than the trap maximalism that defines his hits. Its return is notable mostly for Rihanna's vocal scarcity: she has released music only in fragments since Anti, including the Wakanda Forever ballads "Lift Me Up" and "Born Again," as Entertainment Now notes. Any track carrying her voice draws renewed attention by default.
