Twelve summers on, the definitive salsa anthem of the 2010s is back across the global charts, appearing on 104 country and platform rankings today after a three-week dip. The track is Marc Anthony's salsa reworking of Khaled's 2012 hit C'est la vie, transposed into a horn-driven, call-and-response celebration built for dancefloors — a live-in-the-moment mantra that has functioned as a wedding, graduation, and summer-cookout staple ever since. Anthony, the best-selling salsa artist of all time, delivered its most memorable outing at the 14th Latin GRAMMY Awards in 2013, a performance the Recording Academy still revisits. The song's July release date makes its return to broad rotation each summer a familiar rhythm. Expect the Latin-market numbers to hold through the season.
