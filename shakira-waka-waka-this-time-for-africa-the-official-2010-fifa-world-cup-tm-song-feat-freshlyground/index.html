Shakira, Freshlyground — Waka Waka (This Time for Africa) [The Official 2010 FIFA World Cup (TM) Song] (feat. Freshlyground)

2010 latin pop 🇨🇴 CO

Trending since June 6, 2026 · peaked at 84 charts

The 2010 World Cup anthem is broadly charting across 84 country and platform charts as the 2026 FIFA World Cup cycle gathers force. The trigger is Shakira's new tournament song, Dai Dai, a collaboration with Burna Boy that, per AS USA, has sent listeners back to the track that defined her football legacy. Released as the official song of the South Africa tournament, Waka Waka holds a Guinness record as the most-streamed World Cup song on Spotify, with over a billion streams. Its chorus is built on a two-bar crowd chant, a structural trait Shakira has carried through every host nation — Freshlyground in 2010, Carlinhos Brown in 2014, now Burna Boy. As the new cup nears, the old anthem rises with it.
