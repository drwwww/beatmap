Ten years after it opened Adele's third album, the piano ballad that reintroduced her to the world is broadly charting again, appearing across 100 country and platform charts today after a two-and-a-half-month absence. The lead single from 25, written with producer Greg Kurstin, remains one of the definitive vocal-driven pop ballads of the 2010s — a phone-call-across-time conceit built on restraint until the chorus opens the throttle. It anchors curated soul-ballad playlists alongside Rolling in the Deep, per ad-hoc-news, which keeps Adele's catalog in steady rotation regardless of a release cycle. That durability — the song's ability to resurface without a fresh push — is what returns it to this kind of wide global presence.
