Toygar Işıklı — Anlatamam

(I Cannot Explain) 2016 🇹🇷 TR

Trending since May 15, 2026 · peaked at 140 charts

Composed by Toygar Işıklı for the Turkish drama Kara Sevda (Endless Love), Anlatamam is currently charting across 140 country and platform charts — a striking level of global presence for a piece of Turkish television scoring that never had a conventional pop release.

Işıklı, a Berklee-trained composer who has scored more than 50 Turkish film and television productions, wrote the full soundtrack for Kara Sevda, a series that became one of the most-exported Turkish dramas of the last decade. The show's international syndication footprint — distributed across the Middle East, Latin America, and parts of Europe — gave the score a built-in diaspora audience that western chart-watchers rarely account for. Anlatamam, the track most closely associated with the drama's central love story, has followed that distribution trail.

Işıklı's orchestral-pop construction — spare piano against swelling strings — travels well without lyrics, functioning as emotional shorthand regardless of the listener's language. The track's broad presence today reflects how deeply dizi soundtracks have threaded into global streaming behavior.
