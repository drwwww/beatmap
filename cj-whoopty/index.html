CJ — Whoopty

2020 dembow 🇧🇪 BE

Trending since August 4, 2026 · peaked at 87 charts

Heavy on the SSO shit, man
Loyalty over royalty, y'all niggas know the vibes
Pxcoyo killed this beat, bitch

Five years after it broke, the Brooklyn drill single that made Christopher Soriano Jr. a chart name is back to broad global presence, appearing on 87 country and platform charts after a roughly two-and-a-half-month absence. The track built its case on a Bollywood sample — a lift of Arijit Singh and Mithoon's "Sanam Re," the same source drawn on by Pop Smoke and King Von, per Wikipedia. It peaked at number 3 on the UK Singles Chart and number 10 on the Billboard Hot 100, a drill crossover that landed at the exact moment the sound went mainstream. CJ has struggled to match it since, with subsequent releases falling from half a billion streams to a fraction. This resurgence is the catalog cut carrying the catalog.
