The Script released their new album The User's Guide To Being Human on 14 August, and their 2008 breakthrough ballad is surging back with it — broadly charting today across 79 country and platform charts after a month-long absence. Per Pop Scoop, the Dublin band has now amassed 14 billion streams and six UK number-one albums, and a new release cycle reliably drives listeners back to the catalog that built them. The second single from their self-titled debut, the song about a heartbroken man camping on a street corner became the group's breakthrough transatlantic hit, entering the Billboard Hot 100 and later leaning heavily on TV exposure via CBS's Ghost Whisperer, according to Wikipedia. Its return tracks the album rollout closely.
