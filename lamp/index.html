Lamp — 二十歳の恋

(Love at Twenty) 2008 shibuya-keijapanese indiecity pop 🇯🇵 JP

Trending since June 11, 2026 · peaked at 138 charts

Pressing my lips to a translucent petal

A Japanese indie-pop track built on bossa nova rhythms and the structure of classical tanka poetry is broadly charting today, tracked across 138 country and platform charts after a three-week absence. The eighth cut on Lamp's 2008 album ランプ幻想 (Lamp Fantasy), it was written by guitarist Hiroyo Someya in 2001 as an exercise in fusing Brazilian harmony with Japanese lyrical form, per the artist's own account on X. Someya notes the song has lately found its largest audience abroad — particularly in the United States and China — a reversal of the usual domestic-first trajectory for City Pop-adjacent acts. The genre's hushed, jazz-inflected aesthetic has driven a slow international rediscovery cycle through streaming. Its broad return to the charts fits that pattern.
