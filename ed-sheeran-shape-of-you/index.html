Ed Sheeran — Shape of You

After a week-long gap, the dancehall-inflected lead single from ÷ is back across a wide spread of country and platform charts, tracked on 96 today. Written by Sheeran with Steve Mac and Johnny McDaid, the song was famously the last track added to the album and was first conceived for Little Mix before Sheeran kept it, per Wikipedia. It remains one of the most-streamed songs in Spotify history, a fixture of the platform's all-time top tier (Wikipedia). Its loping marimba hook and half-spoken verses have kept it durable across nearly a decade of playlist rotation. The track is broadly charting again today.
