BTS's return to the stage is dragging their catalog back into global rotation. The septet performed at the FIFA World Cup Final halftime show, and the wave of renewed attention has spread well beyond the setlist, with multiple outlets tracking a streaming jump across performers. Originally a Love Yourself: Her B-side before its Steve Aoki remix, MIC Drop became the group's first single to earn an RIAA Gold certification, per TuneFind. Its swaggering hip-hop boast — every verse a catalog of the group's own achievements — makes it the natural companion piece to a comeback moment. The track is broadly charting today, appearing across 155 country and platform charts as the band's touring cycle reignites their discography.
