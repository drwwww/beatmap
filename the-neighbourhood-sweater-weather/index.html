The Neighbourhood — Sweater Weather

2013 🇺🇸 US

Trending since May 17, 2026 · peaked at 95 charts

And all I am is a man
I want the world in my hands
I hate the beach but I stand
In California with my toes in the sand

The Neighbourhood just wrapped a tour stop at TD Garden, and the crowd sang every word back. The Harvard Crimson reviewed the May 7th Boston date, noting that Jesse Rutherford swapped "California" for "Boston" mid-lyric — the kind of moment that lands on phones and recirculates fast. That live energy is folding into a longer structural story: Sweater Weather has become one of the most-streamed catalog tracks on Spotify, sitting third all-time on the platform behind The Weeknd's Blinding Lights and Ed Sheeran's Shape of You, per BFM TV. The track's climb to that position has been driven in large part by TikTok's sustained rediscovery of the song's melancholic indie-rock atmosphere, as GMX reported earlier this year. Originally the lead single from the band's debut album I Love You, it peaked at number 14 on the Billboard Hot 100 in 2013 — its current global streaming footprint has long since dwarfed that original run. Tracking across 95 country and platform charts today, the song is now less a comeback than a permanent fixture with occasional new peaks.
