Thanks 700-week milestone

Drake, Rihanna — Take Care

2011 rap 🇨🇦 CA

Trending since August 9, 2026 · peaked at 144 charts

I know you've been hurt by someone else
I can tell by the way you carry yourself
If you let me, here's what I'll do
I'll take care of you

The title track from Drake's second album is broadly charting today, tracked across 144 country and platform charts. Built around a Jamie xx remix of Gil Scott-Heron's take on "I'll Take Care of You," the song pairs Drake's confessional register with Rihanna's weary hook — a template that would define a decade of moody rap-and-B, per Wikipedia. Its parent album remains one of the most durable catalog releases in modern hip-hop, having crossed 700 weeks on the Billboard 200, a milestone shared with records by Pink Floyd and Michael Jackson, according to Minute Mirror. It sits in Drake's catalog as the emotional center of the era that made him a global fixture.
