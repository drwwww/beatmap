Don Diablo's future-house reworking of the DJ Snake and Justin Bieber hit is back across the global charts today, appearing on 102 country and platform charts after a 45-day absence. The original — the third single from DJ Snake's Encore, released via Interscope in August 2016 — spawned an entire remix pack in 2018 featuring flips from Tiësto, Marshmello, and the Dutch producer's own uptempo treatment. This version trades the slow-build of Bieber's autotuned vocal for a brighter, four-on-the-floor lift that has aged into a reliable DJ-set staple. The remix's return in broad simultaneous presence across markets tracks the way EDM catalog cuts cycle back through streaming, often years past release.
