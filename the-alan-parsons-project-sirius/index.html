The Alan Parsons Project — Sirius

1982 progressive rockclassic rock 🇬🇧 GB

Trending since June 20, 2026 · last seen July 22, 2026 · peaked at 141 charts

The most recognizable pitch-taking cue in world football is broadly charting today, tracked across 141 country and platform charts after a month-long absence. Designated the official player walk-on music for this summer's global fútbol tournament, the instrumental is getting fresh play worldwide, per Yahoo — but its sporting pedigree runs deep, dating to 1984, when the Chicago Bulls adopted it as their pregame hype track and turned it into shorthand for Michael Jordan's dynasty. The nearly two-minute overture, written by Alan Parsons and Eric Woolfson to open the album Eye in the Sky, has since threaded through everything from The Sopranos to the Deadpool 3 trailer. Recent sync interest has been strong: the piece scored the season-two finale of Severance, according to TikTok. Multiple entry points, one durable swell.
