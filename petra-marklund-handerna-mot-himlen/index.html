Sweden's defining summer singalong is broadly present on the global charts today, tracked across 99 country and platform charts. Released as the lead single from Marklund's fifth album, Inferno, the track was certified 6× platinum by 2015 and has since cleared 118 million Spotify streams, per its Spotify page. Built on a soaring, anthemic chorus sung in Swedish — the artist had previously recorded in English as September — it became the song that recast her as one of the country's most beloved homegrown voices, a fixture of the televised Allsång på Skansen tradition she would later host. Its present resurgence aligns with Sweden's open-air concert season, the annual stretch when communal, hands-in-the-air anthems return to outdoor stages and playlists. The track re-entered after a brief gap.
