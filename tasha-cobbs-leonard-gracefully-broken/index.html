An eight-year-old gospel anthem about surrender is back across the global charts, tracked today on 126 country and platform charts after a two-week absence. Released in August 2017 on Heart. Passion. Pursuit., the song built Tasha Cobbs Leonard's reputation as gospel's premier worship architect, following the breakout of her 2013 single "Break Every Chain." Its theology of brokenness — the idea that fracture is positioning, not punishment — has made it a fixture at funerals, baptisms, and recovery testimonies, and Cobbs Leonard has spoken about the song pulling her through her own depression, per Movieguide. The track's slow-build arrangement, from spoken benediction to full choir, rewards the kind of repeat play that keeps gospel catalog durable. Its broad presence today fits that long, steady life.
