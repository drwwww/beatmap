Reggae's most enduring reassurance is broadly present across the global charts today, appearing on 65 country and platform charts after a three-week dip. Written at Marley's Tuff Gong headquarters at 56 Hope Road — its lyric reportedly prompted by birds fluttering past the window, per uDiscover Music — the song has become a hit several times over across decades, outlasting the album cuts around it. Its afterlife on screen runs deep, from a shopping-scene sync catalogued at Tunefind to its inclusion among the Marley catalog surrounding 2024's biopic Bob Marley: One Love. The three-chord optimism and repeating hook make it endlessly loopable — a comfort standard that resurfaces whenever the world needs one. Its return here fits that pattern.
