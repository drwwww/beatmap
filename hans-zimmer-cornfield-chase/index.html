Hans Zimmer — Cornfield Chase

2014 soundtrackscore 🇺🇸 US

Trending since May 19, 2026 · last seen July 10, 2026 · peaked at 140 charts

Eleven years after it underscored a father's escape through a dust-choked cornfield, Christopher Nolan's Interstellar score cut is broadly charting again, tracked across 140 country and platform charts today. The revival runs through short-form video, where slowed-and-reverb edits of the track have become the default bed for space-and-time montages on TikTok — clips pairing the arpeggiated organ swell with footage of Earth, cosmic vistas, and film re-cuts, as documented across TikTok discovery pages. The piece, from the Interstellar original soundtrack, has outgrown its source: its minimalist build translates cleanly to a loop, and its emotional register — awe braided with dread — fits the platform's appetite for the sublime. Zimmer, a two-time Academy Award winner per Wikipedia, needs no promotion for it. The track's return follows a 30-day absence from broad presence; watch whether the edits sustain it.
