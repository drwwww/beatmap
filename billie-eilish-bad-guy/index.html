Seven years on, the song that made Billie Eilish a household name is back in broad rotation, tracked across 95 country and platform charts today after a stretch off the radar. The whisper-sung, finger-snap-and-sub-bass single — built with her brother Finneas — became the first by an artist born in the 21st century to top the Billboard Hot 100 and reach Diamond certification, per Tunefind. It remains the cornerstone of When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go?, the 2019 debut whose deliberately small, claustrophobic production reset the template for a generation of bedroom-pop. Eilish's Hit Me Hard and Soft tour continues to keep her catalog circulating, with a trailer leaning on that same dark visual language. A reliable returner whenever her name re-enters the conversation.
