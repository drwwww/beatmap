The song that closed out Euphoria's first season is broadly charting again, appearing across 41 country and platform charts after a month-long absence. The renewed attention tracks with the show's return to the news cycle: per Deadline, Euphoria landed Emmy recognition among the year's departing series, and Zendaya has said publicly she believes the coming third season will be its last. Written and produced by Labrinth, the track debuted in the finale, "And Salt the Earth Behind You," as the score to Rue's climactic gospel-tinged fantasy sequence — a Primetime Emmy-winning single, per Tunefind. Its slow-build orchestration and Zendaya's in-character vocal have kept it a signature of the series' sound. Momentum should hold as the Season 3 finality narrative sharpens.
