Rihanna — Cheers (Drink To That)

2010 🇺🇸 US

Trending since May 15, 2026 · peaked at 132 charts

Takin' shots in here, you want one?
Yeah-e-yeah, yeah-e-yeah, yeah-e-yeah

The Loud party cut is broadly charting today across 132 country and platform charts, a steady presence for a track that has outlasted its album cycle by fifteen years. Built around a sample of Avril Lavigne's "I'm with You" and a BBC Music-described "funky, loping guitar groove," the song was Rihanna's explicit ode to weekend-release culture — she dedicated it, in her words, to "all the semi-alcoholics in the world." That directness is probably why it holds. Where other Loud singles leaned into maximalist production, this one is relatively spare, built for bars and playlists over headphones and critical listening. It sits in a comfortable lane alongside "We Found Love" and "Only Girl" as reliable catalog draws rather than prestige picks. Rihanna recently crossed 200 million RIAA singles certifications across her catalog — a milestone that has lifted streaming numbers on deep cuts and familiar ones alike.
