Patience Dabany — Allo allo

2019 bikutsicoupé décalérumba congolaise 🇬🇦 GA

Trending since May 15, 2026 · peaked at 133 charts

Gabonese singer Patience Dabany is broadly charting today across 133 country and platform charts, a notable spread for an artist whose catalog has circulated largely within Francophone African music communities. Dabany — born Marie-Joëlle Engohang-Ndong, and the former first lady of Gabon — has been recording since the 1980s, blending Afro-rumba and Congolese soukous with French-language pop songwriting. Allo allo, released on her 2019 album L'amour d'une mère, sits in that tradition: warm, melodic, and built around the kind of understated vocal delivery that travels well across generations of Central and West African popular taste. The track is distributed through Believe and remains available across major streaming platforms including Apple Music and Anghami.
