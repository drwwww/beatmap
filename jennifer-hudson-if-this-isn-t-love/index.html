Jennifer Hudson's selection to sing the U.S. national anthem at the 2026 World Cup final has pulled her catalog back into circulation, and this 2008 debut-album single is the beneficiary — appearing on 52 country and platform charts today after a months-long absence. Both MARCA and Yahoo Sports name If This Isn't Love among her signature tracks, alongside Spotlight, in the run-up to the appearance. The uptempo Brian Kennedy production, written by the Thomas brothers of Planet VI, was also featured on the Sex and the City: The Movie soundtrack in 2008. A high-visibility live performance reliably lifts an artist's back catalog; this is the mechanism working in real time.
