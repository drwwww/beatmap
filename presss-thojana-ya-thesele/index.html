A gqom anthem out of South Africa is back across the global charts today, appearing on 20 country and platform rankings after a five-week absence. Built on deep, hypnotic beats and shout-along chants, the track sits squarely in the tradition that carried South African dance music from Durban taproom parties into international rotation — citiMuzik frames it as a street-party staple driven by unfiltered energy and catchy hooks. Released in 2014 on the album Black, it trades melody for momentum: chanted vocals over relentless low-end, the kind of record that lives in physical spaces before it lives on playlists. Its return to broad presence marks the second such swell in recent weeks. The gqom discovery cycle runs on word of mouth more than promotion — worth tracking where it lands next.
