Gorod 312 — Останусь

(I Will Stay) 2006

Trending since May 15, 2026 · peaked at 134 charts

В конце туннеля яркий свет слепой звезды
Подошвы на сухой листве оставят следы
Еще под кожей бъётся пульс и надо жить
Я больше может не вернусь

Twenty years after its release, this Kazakhstani pop-rock ballad is broadly charting today across 134 country and platform charts. Gorod 312 — a group from Almaty whose name translates roughly as "City 312," the old Soviet telephone code for the city — broke through across Russian-language markets in the mid-2000s with a sound that sat somewhere between post-Soviet guitar pop and the melodic rock that dominated Eastern European radio at the time. Останусь was their signature: a slow-burning, minor-key meditation on departure and the pull to stay, built around a restrained verse that opens into a wide, anthemic refrain. The lyric's imagery — a light at the tunnel's end, footprints in dry leaves, a pulse still beating under the skin — gave the song a durability that outlasted its chart cycle. It remains one of the most-searched Russian-language tracks of its era on Spotify, where multiple versions (radio edit, triplex version) are currently in circulation.
