Rag'n'Bone Man — Human

The breakthrough single that built Rory Graham's career is broadly charting today, appearing across 92 country and platform charts after a brief absence. Released through Columbia in 2016 and anchoring a 2017 debut that, per Tunefind, became the fastest-selling debut album by a male artist in the UK that decade, the song pairs Graham's deep baritone with a sparse, blues-gospel arrangement and a hook built for confession. Its sync history runs deep: a trailer for Marvel's Inhumans, the Netflix documentary 13th, the BioWare game Mass Effect: Andromeda, and the season-two intro to Into the Badlands. That soundtrack ubiquity has kept the track in rotation long past its release cycle, and it remains a durable streaming fixture nearly a decade on.
