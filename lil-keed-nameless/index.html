Lil Keed — Nameless

Trending since July 27, 2026 · peaked at 144 charts

The melodic breakout of the late Atlanta rapper is broadly charting today, tracked across 144 country and platform charts after a week's absence. "Nameless" was the lead single from Keed Talk to 'Em, the first mixtape Lil Keed recorded after signing to Young Thug's Young Stoner Life imprint, and it reached number 42 on the Billboard Hot 100 on release. Over a dreamy beat, Keed's staccato, half-sung flows carry a slippery love song — HotNewHipHop's Alex Zidel singled it out via Wikipedia as one of the tape's strongest cuts. Keed died in May 2022 at 24, and the track has remained his most durable calling card, the clearest distillation of the loose, tuneful style he pulled from his YSL mentors.
