One Direction — Story of My Life

2013

Trending since August 13, 2026 · peaked at 82 charts

Written in these walls are the stories that I can't explain
I leave my heart open but it stays right here empty for days
She told me in the morning she don't feel the same about us in her bones
It seems to me that when I die these words will be written on my stone

One Direction's most enduring ballad is broadly charting again, tracked across 82 country and platform charts a month after slipping out of view. The second single from Midnight Memories — a folk-leaning acoustic turn co-written by all five members — has held cultural weight the group's uptempo singles never quite matched; its official video crossed a billion YouTube views, a milestone Billboard marked in 2023. The song has also become a grief anthem in the wake of Liam Payne's death in October 2024, a loss that reunited the surviving members publicly, per the One Direction Wiki. Its return to such wide presence tracks with a fanbase that keeps circling back to this particular chorus.
