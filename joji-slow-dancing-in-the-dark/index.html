Joji's signature ballad is back across 25 country and platform charts, tracking with his ongoing Solaris Tour. At a recent Charlotte stop, the singer closed the night on an alternative arrangement of the song that made his 2018 mainstream breakthrough, per The Concert Chronicles — the kind of live moment that reliably sends catalog streams back up. The former Filthy Frank internet personality reinvented himself through 88rising as a purveyor of woozy, heartbroken R&B, and this track remains the emotional center of that pivot. It has a long sync history to match, appearing in Euphoria and the medical drama The Resident. The reappearance follows a 26-day absence from broad charting.
