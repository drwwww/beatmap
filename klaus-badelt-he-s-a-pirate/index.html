Klaus Badelt — He's a Pirate

2003 soundtrack 🇺🇸 US

Trending since June 27, 2026 · peaked at 63 charts

The orchestral theme that scores the end credits of every Pirates of the Caribbean film is broadly charting today, tracked across 63 country and platform charts after a five-week absence. Composed with Hans Zimmer for Pirates of the Caribbean: The Curse of the Black Pearl, the piece has long outgrown its source — a galloping string-and-brass motif that functions as shorthand for adventure across short-form video, gaming streams, and sports arenas. Per Wikipedia, the Tampa Bay Buccaneers have used a cut version as pre-game and time-out music at Raymond James Stadium for years. Badelt took the assignment when Zimmer was occupied with The Last Samurai, then watched the 90-second cue become one of the most durable film themes of the 2000s. Its return to broad presence today fits a track that resurfaces in cycles rather than retiring.
