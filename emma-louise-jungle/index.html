A wistful Australian indie-pop ballad with a Wankelmut remix in its DNA is broadly present across the global charts today, appearing on 41 country and platform charts. The song has a long second life behind it: per Wikipedia, it soundtracked season 8, episode 2 of Grey's Anatomy and went viral in January 2022 as the "My Head Is a Jungle" trend, the hook that turned a 2011 single into recurring short-form fodder. Yves Saint Laurent licensed it for the worldwide Black Opium campaign in 2014, and Emma Louise herself parlayed the profile into a featured turn on Flight Facilities' "Two Bodies." The track's reedy vocal and slow-build dynamics have made it durable sync material for over a decade. Its current spread suggests another short-form cycle is underway.
