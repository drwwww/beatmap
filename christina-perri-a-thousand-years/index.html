A wedding-aisle standard from the Twilight era is back across the global charts after a three-week dip, appearing on 16 country and platform charts today. The trigger is television: Perri's ballad featured prominently in the third season of Shrinking, with the singer also performing it alongside the show's cast, per Wikipedia. The song was written with producer David Hodges for The Twilight Saga: Breaking Dawn – Part 1, where it played over the credits before fans adopted it as the eternal-love theme for Bella and Edward's romance — a usage Teen Vogue traces to the soundtrack's release. Certified Diamond by the RIAA, it has rarely left rotation at weddings and first dances. The new sync renews a track that has spent fifteen years as a fixture of romantic montages.
