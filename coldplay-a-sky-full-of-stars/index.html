Coldplay — A Sky Full of Stars

Chris Martin closed the FIFA World Cup Final Halftime Show with a performance that put this 2014 single back in front of a global audience, and the track is broadly charting today across 71 country and platform rankings after a three-week absence. Per FOX Sports, the set ended on the song's euphoric build — the kind of stadium-scale singalong Coldplay engineered it to be. The Avicii co-production was the second single from Ghost Stories, a departure from the album's muted grief into outright EDM catharsis. It has also carried a second life in cinema, including a rendition in Sing 2. A televised event this large tends to spike the whole catalog; this is the cut that spiked hardest.
