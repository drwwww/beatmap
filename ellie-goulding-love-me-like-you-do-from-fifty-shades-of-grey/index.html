Ten years after it anchored the soundtrack to Fifty Shades of Grey, Goulding's soaring ballad is broadly charting again, appearing across 43 country and platform charts after a nearly two-month absence. Written by a heavyweight team including Max Martin, Tove Lo and Savan Kotecha, the track was engineered as a swelling, single-emotion set piece — all crescendo, minimal architecture — the kind of build that reads instantly as romantic climax. That legibility has kept it in steady rotation on TikTok, where it soundtracks more than 396,000 clips. With over 1.8 billion Spotify streams, it remains the commercial high-water mark of Goulding's catalog and one of the more durable pop-cinema pairings of the last decade. The re-entry is worth monitoring for whether it holds past a single snapshot.
