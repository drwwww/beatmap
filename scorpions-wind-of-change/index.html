Klaus Meine's whistled power ballad — the unofficial soundtrack to the fall of the Berlin Wall — has returned to the global charts today after a 20-day gap, tracked across 132 country and platform charts. Written after the band played Moscow in 1989, the track reached No. 4 on the Billboard Hot 100 in 1991 and remains one of the best-selling singles of all time, its Cold War imagery hardening into a generational symbol of détente. Its afterlife has stayed active: a placement in Call of Duty: Black Ops's "First Strike" trailer, an appearance in the Chuck episode "Versus the Seduction Impossible," and a 2020 podcast investigation into whether the CIA, not the Scorpions, actually wrote it. That whistle still carries.
