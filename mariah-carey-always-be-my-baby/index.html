Carey's third single from Daydream is broadly charting today, tracked across 16 country and platform rankings. The song became her 11th Billboard Hot 100 number one, a run that tied her with Whitney Houston at the time and helped cement her position as the most chart-dominant soloist of the era — she now holds 19 Hot 100 chart-toppers, per Wikipedia. Built around a humming phrase Carey first sang to producers Jermaine Dupri and Manuel Seal, the track pairs a featherlight vocal with a midtempo R&B shuffle that has aged into a summer staple. Fifth Harmony heavily sampled it on their 2015 debut cut Like Mariah, and the song remains one of the most durable entries in her catalog.
