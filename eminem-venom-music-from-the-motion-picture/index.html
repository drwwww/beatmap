Eminem's late-period rap-rock hybrid is broadly charting today, tracked across 129 country and platform charts. Written for the 2018 Marvel film about Eddie Brock and his alien symbiote, the track closed out Kamikaze, the surprise album Eminem released as a rebuke to critics of Revival — and it has since become one of his most durable streaming workhorses, propelled by a hook built for short-form clipping. Per TikTok, the sound now anchors over a million videos, much of it lyric-overlay and edit content keyed to the song's bludgeoning final verse. The track's structure — all momentum, minimal melody, a chorus that lands like a threat — is precisely the kind of material that loops cleanly under a movie edit. Its presence across so many markets at once is the snapshot worth noting.
