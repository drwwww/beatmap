After a 46-day absence, the electro-house landmark is back and broadly charting today, tracked across 119 country and platform charts. Built on MacinTalk speech synthesizers repeating a single suggestive phrase over a buzzsaw bassline, the track helped define the electro-house template that dominated club music through the mid-2000s, as documented on Wikipedia. Its cultural footprint runs deep: the song appears in the comedy White Chicks, soundtracks Just Dance and DJ Hero, and was later interpolated by Flo Rida's "Touch Me." The music video itself surfaces, silent, in the opening montage of Don Jon. Its return to such a wide chart spread suggests the sleazy synth hook still translates cleanly to a new generation of short-form clips.
