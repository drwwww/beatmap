Yiruma — River Flows In You

2001 neoclassical 🇰🇷 KR

Trending since July 2, 2026 · peaked at 87 charts

A quarter-century-old solo piano piece by South Korean composer Yiruma is broadly charting today, tracked across 87 country and platform charts after a seven-week absence. Written for his 2001 album First Love, the track's recurring, repetitive melodic patterns have long been misattributed to Twilight — a myth Classic FM traces to the song's ubiquity in early-2010s teen media, though it never actually appeared in the films, as IMDb notes. Its durability owes more to function than fashion: it has become a default wedding-ceremony piece, a staple for aspiring pianists, and a shorthand for tasteful ambient calm. The composition itself was inspired by Samuel Vallée's 1999 "Une rivière coule en toi." It sits comfortably in the new-age contemporary-classical lane alongside Ludovico Einaudi — the exact register that streaming's focus and study playlists reward.
