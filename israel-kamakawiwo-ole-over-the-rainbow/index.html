Israel Kamakawiwo'ole — Over the Rainbow

2001

Trending since June 28, 2026 · peaked at 119 charts

The ukulele medley that has soundtracked a generation of memorials and montages is broadly charting today, appearing across 119 country and platform charts after a near-month absence. Recorded at 3 a.m. in a Honolulu studio in 1988 and first released on the 1993 album Facing Future — the first Hawaiian album to top one million in U.S. sales, per Mix — IZ's gentle reworking of the Judy Garland standard has had an unusually long second life. It entered the UK charts in 2007 and climbed to No. 46, partly driven by syncs including an Axe deodorant ad, according to Wikipedia. Its 52 documented film and TV appearances, logged by Tunefind, keep it perpetually in rotation — most recently across episodes of The Afterparty and Magnum P.I. The song endures because it sounds like comfort.
