Brazil's most successful girl group is back on the global charts today, its debut single tracked across 41 country and platform charts after a month-long absence. Rouge formed in 2002 out of the first season of Brazil's Popstars adaptation, and this was the lead single from their self-titled debut — a Portuguese reworking of "Irresistible," originally written in English by Kara DioGuardi, the songwriter later known as an American Idol judge. The track pairs an early-2000s dance-pop pulse with a slow-burn hook about resisting attraction. Its return follows the enduring nostalgia machine around Rouge, whose reunions and streaming afterlife have kept the catalog in rotation among Brazilian listeners. The song's broad presence today marks its steadiest showing in weeks.
