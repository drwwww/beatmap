A decade-old altar-call anthem from Charlotte's Elevation Church is broadly charting today, tracked across 132 country and platform charts. Written by Chris Brown, Mack Brock, Steven Furtick, and Wade Joye for the live album Here as in Heaven, the song built its reach the slow way — a modern church standard that spread through Sunday services long before streaming numbers caught up, per Singersroom. Its structure is built for congregational singing: a plainspoken verse posing a question, a wide-open chorus designed to be sung back. The collective, an extension of the church's music ministry since 2007, has moved over 1.5 million units, according to Wikipedia. Its durability rests on function — this is music written to be used, not just heard.
