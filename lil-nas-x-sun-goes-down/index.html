Five years to the week after its release, SUN GOES DOWN is broadly charting across 42 country and platform charts. The song dropped on May 21, 2021 as the second single from Montero, Lil Nas X's debut album, and where that project's title track leaned into provocation, this one turned inward — Lil Nas X addressing his younger, closeted self, reflecting on bullying, self-doubt, and suicidal ideation as a Black teenager in Georgia. It remains among the rawest pieces of writing in his catalog, and the one most likely to resurface on personal anniversaries and mental-health awareness cycles. The Wikipedia entry for the song notes that the music video extends the narrative, dramatizing those adolescent struggles in detail. Re-entries like this one — back after an eight-day gap — are a recurring pattern for the track, which has always traveled more on emotional resonance than on radio play.
