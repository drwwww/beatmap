Geek Music — Narcos - Main Theme

2018 anime

Trending since August 6, 2026 · peaked at 112 charts

A cover of the melody that opens Netflix's Narcos is broadly charting today, appearing across 112 country and platform charts — a resurgence that traces back to renewed short-form circulation of the show's opening theme. The original, Rodrigo Amarante's "Tuyo", scores the series about Pablo Escobar and the Medellín Cartel; this Geek Music instrumental rendition rides the same nostalgia. Its climb has been fed by a steady run of TikTok edits — Narcos scene cuts, piano covers, and Pedro Pascal montages — that lean on the theme's brooding bolero guitar as atmospheric shorthand for menace. Geek Music, a catalog outfit specializing in soundtrack recreations, released this version in July 2018 via Symphonic Distribution. The track's presence across so many markets at once suggests the Narcos audio moment has spread well past its Latin American core.
