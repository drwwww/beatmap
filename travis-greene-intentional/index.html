Travis Greene — Intentional

2015 gospelworship

Trending since August 10, 2026 · peaked at 126 charts

A decade-old gospel standard from Travis Greene is broadly charting today, tracked across 126 country and platform charts after a six-week absence. The likeliest driver is proximity: Greene is touring South Africa, where fans are being primed to "sing every line at the top of your voice" across a setlist built on Intentional, "Made a Way," and "You Waited," per a promotional clip circulating on TikTok. The song itself is a slow-building worship anthem from his album The Hill — a repeated affirmation ("All things are working for my good") that resolves into the title refrain, structured more like a congregational chant than a pop single. That build is what makes it durable in live rooms. With over 31 million Spotify streams on the original, its return to broad presence tracks a live cycle rather than a sync.
