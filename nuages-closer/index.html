A drifting instrumental beat from the early-2010s SoundCloud underground is broadly charting today, tracked across 42 country and platform charts after a 19-day absence. Built around a chopped, pitched vocal loop and a heavy-lidded low end, Closer belongs to the wave of producers who turned chillwave and lo-fi hip-hop into ambient background listening — music made for studying, scrolling, and falling asleep. Its current life runs through slowed-and-reverb edits and rain-ASMR uploads that stretch the track into long, hazy loops, the kind built to soundtrack relaxation videos rather than dance floors. The original, released June 2013, samples Karen O's "Igloo," per Genius. Its return after nearly three weeks off-chart suggests the resurgence has staying power among ambient-leaning listeners.
