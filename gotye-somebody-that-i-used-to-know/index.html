Gotye, Kimbra — Somebody That I Used To Know

The Record of the Year Grammy winner is broadly charting again, appearing across 56 country and platform charts after a stretch off the radar. Fifteen years on, the Gotye-Kimbra duet remains the definitive one-hit-wonder case study — a xylophone-driven breakup ballad that turned the Belgian-Australian musician into a global name off a single song, then let him recede almost entirely. The track's durability owes to its structure: a call-and-response argument between two ex-lovers, each verse handing the microphone to the other side of the wreckage. A TikTok "then vs. now" reimagining format — pairing the original body-paint video with a hypothetical 2026 recast — has been circulating, keeping the song's imagery in rotation. It has weathered Glee covers and countless parodies without losing its edge.
