Tony Anderson — Butterflies - Piano Sonata

2018 🇺🇸 US

Trending since May 29, 2026 · last seen June 14, 2026 · peaked at 118 charts

A solo-piano reverie from a film-and-television composer is broadly charting today, tracked across 118 country and platform charts after a brief gap in its run. Tony Anderson, a Florida-born composer who works mostly in score and trailer music for film and TV, released the piece in January 2018 as the first installment of a planned two-part "snowfall interpretation" of his earlier track Butterflies. The sonata is exactly what its title promises — spare, unhurried, built for emotional underscore rather than the radio — which is why it keeps drawing filmmakers. Director Logan Marshall used it for a short visual interpretation following a young couple from first meeting through cohabitation, the kind of wordless sync work that has quietly defined the composition's afterlife. Its current breadth across the global charts marks the latest chapter in that slow circulation.
