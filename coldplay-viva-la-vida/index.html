Coldplay — Viva La Vida

2008 🇬🇧 GB

Trending since June 12, 2026 · peaked at 141 charts

I used to rule the world
Seas would rise when I gave the word
Now in the morning, I sleep alone
Sweep the streets I used to own

Eighteen years to the day after its parent album arrived, Viva La Vida is broadly present across the global charts, tracked across 141 country and platform charts after an eight-day absence. The orchestral, string-driven anthem was Coldplay's commercial high-water mark — their first song to top the Billboard Hot 100, the first single from a British act to hit number one in the US and UK simultaneously since Rod Stewart in 1971, and the Grammy Song of the Year in 2009. The June 12 anniversary of Viva la Vida or Death and All His Friends lines up neatly with today's surge. The track has stayed in cultural rotation for years, from its Super Bowl 50 halftime performance to a sync in the final episode of Suits. Few catalog cuts return this wide.
