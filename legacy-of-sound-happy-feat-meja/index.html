Legacy Of Sound, Meja — Happy (feat. Meja)

1993 eurodance 🇸🇪 SE

Trending since August 3, 2026 · peaked at 127 charts

A house-inflected pop single from the early '90s Swedish dance scene is broadly charting today, tracked across 127 country and platform charts. Built around Meja's clear, elastic vocal — the same voice that would later go solo with "All 'Bout the Money" — the track pairs a buoyant piano-house arrangement with a hook engineered for radio, a hallmark of the Stockholm production ethos that would soon export Robyn, Ace of Base, and the Cheiron hit factory to the world. Legacy of Sound was a short-lived project, and this was its calling card. What has surfaced online centers on the group's contemporaneous single "I Can't Let You Go," which has its own 1993 music video credited to director Patrik Blomberg.
