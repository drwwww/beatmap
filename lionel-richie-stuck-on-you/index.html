Lionel Richie — Stuck On You

1983 motown 🇺🇸 US

Trending since August 17, 2026 · peaked at 92 charts

A country-tinged Motown ballad from Richie's blockbuster Can't Slow Down is broadly charting today, tracked across 92 country and platform charts after a two-month lull. One of the softer cuts from an album otherwise built on the dancefloor propulsion of All Night Long, the song leaned into pedal steel and a plainspoken devotional lyric, a deliberate crossover move that earned it top-ten placements on both sides of the Atlantic, per Wikipedia. Father-son lip-sync covers have kept the track circulating on TikTok, where its unhurried sway suits the platform's slow-throwback register. Its durability owes to that simplicity — a hook that states its case and never overreaches. Richie, still touring and a fixture on American Idol, keeps the catalog in permanent rotation.
