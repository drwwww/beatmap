A live recording from Argentine rock elder Indio Solari has surged across the charts, appearing on 148 country and platform charts today against a recent average closer to nine. Solari, the former frontman of Patricio Rey y sus Redonditos de Ricota, is one of the defining voices of Argentine rock and carries a devotional following rare even by stadium-act standards. This particular cut — backed by his Los Fundamentalistas del Aire Acondicionado band and released as a single in May 2021 — captures him in performance, its lyric folding back on itself from ending to beginning. The track is built on the elliptical, literary songwriting that made him a cult figure across the Río de la Plata. Today it sits firmly in the broad-charting tier.
