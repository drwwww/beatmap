Thanks Kennedy tribute

The Cranberries — Zombie

2008 🇮🇪 IE

Trending since May 18, 2026 · last seen August 3, 2026 · peaked at 142 charts

Another head hangs lowly
Child is slowly taken

A Dermot Kennedy tribute at Dublin's Aviva Stadium has pushed the Cranberries' fiercest anthem back into broad circulation, appearing on 30 charts today after a stretch of dormancy. Kennedy brought Noel and Mike Hogan onstage for a performance of the song, according to RTÉ — a moment freighted with meaning given that Dolores O'Riordan, who wrote the track about young victims of a 1993 Warrington bombing during the Troubles, died in 2018. The 1994 protest anthem has long outlived its moment: its music video became the first by an Irish band to pass a billion YouTube views, per Tunefind. It has soundtracked Yellowjackets and Zack Snyder's Army of the Dead. Kennedy's onstage reunion with the surviving Hogan brothers gave a grieving fanbase a reason to return.
