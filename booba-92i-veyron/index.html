A 2025 remix has pushed one of French rap's hardest catalog cuts back into broad rotation, and 92i veyron is charting across 89 country and platform lists today after a seven-week absence. The remix single, released this year via Apple Music, has returned attention to the original from Booba's 2015 album Nero Nemesis — the track whose title splices 92i, the name of Booba's Hauts-de-Seine crew and label, with the ultra-rare Bugatti it name-checks. Its resurgence also tracks with Booba's 2026 live run, where Basket-Mag counts the song among the set pieces — alongside "G5," "DKR" and "Boulbi" — that arenas of fans deliver back word for word. The looped "Personne, personne" hook and menace-laced verses remain a template for the French street-rap Booba helped define. Watch the remix's traction into August.
