The James Horner-scored Titanic centerpiece is back across the global charts today, appearing on 28 country and platform charts after a roughly two-month absence. Written for James Cameron's 1997 film and released on Dion's album Let's Talk About Love, the track has amassed over 717 million streams on Spotify alone — a durability rooted in Horner's tin-whistle melody and Dion's escalating final-key-change delivery, a belted crescendo engineered for maximum emotional lift. It remains among the best-selling singles ever recorded, and one of the few late-'90s power ballads to have kept a foothold in streaming rather than sliding into nostalgia-only rotation. Its return to broad presence follows a familiar pattern for the song: it recedes, then resurfaces, without fanfare.
