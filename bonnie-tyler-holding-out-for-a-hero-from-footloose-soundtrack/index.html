Bonnie Tyler — Holding Out for a Hero - From "Footloose" Soundtrack

1986 🇬🇧 GB

Trending since July 10, 2026 · peaked at 32 charts

Where have all the good men gone
And where are all the gods?
Where's the streetwise Hercules
To fight the rising odds?

Bonnie Tyler, the Welsh vocalist behind Total Eclipse of the Heart, died at 75 on July 9 in Portugal, following emergency surgery that left her in a coma, ABC News reported. Her death has returned this Jim Steinman–penned power ballad to broad circulation, tracked across 32 country and platform charts today after a two-month absence. Written for the 1984 Footloose and used in the film's climactic sequence, the track drew a tribute from Kevin Bacon himself among the memorials, per the Daily Mail. Catherine Zeta-Jones, a relative, recalled Tyler singing at her wedding. Steinman's overwrought Wagnerian scale — the same wall-of-sound bombast he built for Meat Loaf — is what has kept the song synced and covered for four decades. Expect Total Eclipse to climb alongside it.
