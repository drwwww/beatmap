Peter, Paul and Mary — 500 Miles

1962 folkfolk rock 🇦🇹 AT

Trending since June 21, 2026 · peaked at 44 charts

If you miss the train I'm on, you will know that I am gone
You can hear the whistle blow a hundred miles

A cornerstone of the 1960s American folk revival is broadly charting today, tracked across 44 country and platform charts after a seven-week absence. Built on a traveler's lament — far from home, out of money, too ashamed to return — the trio's reading appeared on their 1962 self-titled debut, an album Wikipedia notes spent ten months in the Billboard Top Ten. Paul Stookey once described it simply as "a reflection of loneliness." Its durability owes much to placement: the song has scored the Australian crime series Mr Inbetween and the 2011 Canadian documentary Rocky Mountain Express, and now circulates on TikTok under emotional film edits, frequently paired with disaster drama The Impossible. The plaintive harmony that made it a civil-rights-era standard reads, six decades on, as pure melancholy.
