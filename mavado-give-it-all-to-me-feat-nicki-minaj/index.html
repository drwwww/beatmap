A DJ Khaled-affiliated dancehall cut from 2014 is back across 39 country and platform charts today after a month away, having last registered broad presence in early July. The track pairs Jamaican singjay Mavado — one of dancehall's most durable voices — with a Nicki Minaj feature from the peak of her crossover run, when she was moving fluidly between rap, pop, and Caribbean registers. Built on a We the Best riddim and Minaj's toasting-adjacent verse, it's a rare linkup that let two distinct star systems, Kingston and Queens, share a hook without either flattening the other. The song's patois-forward chorus has kept it in rotation as party-set filler and short-form soundtrack for over a decade. Its return to broad charting is the observable move worth logging.
