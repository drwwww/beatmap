Brian Tyler — Formula 1 Theme

2018 soundtrackscore 🇺🇸 US

Trending since August 17, 2026 · peaked at 107 charts

The orchestral fanfare that opens every Formula 1 broadcast is broadly charting again, appearing across 107 country and platform charts as the 2026 racing calendar heads into its European summer stretch. Composed by Hollywood's Brian Tyler and recorded with the London Philharmonic Orchestra at Air Studios, the piece became the sport's very first official theme in 2018, and has soundtracked the series's opening titles across 86 episodes of its television coverage — a signature cue now also used in Formula 2 and Formula 3, per Tunefind. Its return tracks the rhythm of the race weekends themselves: the theme resurfaces on charts each time global viewership spikes around a grand prix. The brass-heavy crescendo has also fed a steady stream of metal and electric-cello covers on TikTok. A seasonal pulse, tied to the sport's own calendar.
