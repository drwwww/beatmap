Justin Bieber, Nicki Minaj — Beauty And A Beat

2012

Trending since May 11, 2026 · last seen June 12, 2026 · peaked at 1,102 charts

A 2012 Believe-era single is back atop the global rankings, the engine being Bieber's Coachella reset. Per the Guardian, streams of his catalog nearly tripled the week of his first set in Indio, pulling four songs onto the Hot 100. The Nicki Minaj collaboration has been the chief beneficiary: Billboard clocked it at No. 1 on the Global 200 with 59.2 million weekly streams, holding the position for a second consecutive week. Written by Max Martin, Savan Kotecha and Zedd, the track was always a sleek electro-pop machine; its half-sung hook and Minaj's giddy verse translate cleanly to a streaming-era resurgence. Today it appears across 127 country and platform charts, more than triple its recent weekly average — a clear ripple from the Bieber renaissance narrative now driving his deep catalog.
