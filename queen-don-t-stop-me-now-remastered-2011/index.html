Forty-seven years after its release on the album Jazz, Freddie Mercury's runaway falsetto sprint is broadly charting again, appearing across 141 country and platform charts after a six-week absence. The track has spent decades accruing afterlives in film and television: its bar scene placement in Shaun of the Dead and a fight sequence in Hardcore Henry turned it into shorthand for adrenaline-on-cue, while a Top Gear viewer poll once crowned it the greatest driving song. The 2011 remaster — issued for Queen's 40th anniversary alongside the band's full reissued catalog — is the version now circulating on streaming. Built on Mercury's galloping piano and one of the most propulsive choruses in the catalog, it remains the group's most durable feel-good engine. Summer playlist season tends to bring it back.
