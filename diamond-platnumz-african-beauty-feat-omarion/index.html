Diamond Platnumz, Omarion — African Beauty (feat. Omarion)

2018 bongo flavabongo pianoamapiano 🇹🇿 TZ

Trending since July 8, 2026 · peaked at 65 charts

An eight-year-old Bongo Flava collaboration is broadly charting again, tracked across 65 country and platform charts after a two-month absence. The pairing itself remains the draw: Diamond Platnumz, the Tanzanian superstar and founder of WCB Wasafi, built the track around a lush Afropop groove and handed a verse to Omarion, the former B2K frontman — a rare direct handshake between East African pop and American R&B. That cross-continental novelty is what has kept the song durable, and it accounts for the very broad presence it holds today, spread across markets far beyond its home region. The song sits among the most-streamed cuts in Platnumz's catalog, an early flagship for his push toward international collaborations. Its return follows the pattern of his back-catalog, which resurfaces in waves as his profile widens.
