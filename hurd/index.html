A ballad from Mongolia's longest-running rock band is back across the global charts after a 24-day absence, tracked on 87 country and platform charts today. The lyric's imagery of spring rain and ink-blurred love notes (Musixmatch) explains its durability as a graduation-season and reunion staple. The official video lives on the band's YouTube channel. Its return aligns with the early-summer school-leaving stretch, when the song historically resurfaces.
