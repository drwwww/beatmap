Odell's debut single is broadly charting again, tracked across 82 country and platform charts as the songwriter steps back into the spotlight. The renewed presence coincides with the June 17 release of Old Lovers, a collaboration with The Lumineers that has sent listeners back through his catalog — and back to the piano ballad that started it. That 2012 track has long had a second life on TikTok as a solidarity anthem for Ukraine, a usage that helped push it past four billion Spotify plays. Its desolate verses and breaking falsetto have made it a durable shorthand for grief well beyond its original context. The song has also threaded through television over the years, including a placement in Elementary. The new single is the engine here.
