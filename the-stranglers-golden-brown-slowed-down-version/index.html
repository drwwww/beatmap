The pitched-down edit of the Stranglers' harpsichord-driven signature is broadly charting today, tracked across nine country and platform charts after a stretch off the radar. The driver is short-form video: the slowed treatment has become a bed for medieval and dark-fantasy edits on TikTok — knight montages, cinematic intros — with Buffer noting the version is still pulling new listeners on Instagram more than four decades on. Originally the second single from 1981's La folie, "Golden Brown" climbed to number two in Britain and became the band's biggest hit, its waltzing harpsichord figure an outlier in the punk-adjacent new wave scene the Stranglers helped define. The slowed cut trades the original's brightness for something hazier — better suited to the atmospheric visuals now carrying it.
