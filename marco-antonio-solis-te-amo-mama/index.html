The calendar is doing the work here. A grupera ballad addressed directly to a mother has surged back onto the global charts in the first days of June, riding the long tail of Mother's Day — observed across most of Latin America on May 10 and in the United States on May 11. Billboard files the track among its standing Mother's Day canon, a gratitude letter built around sacrifice and unconditional love. It is back on 95 country and platform charts after an 18-day dormancy, the kind of seasonal re-entry that defines the song's afterlife. Solís, the architect of Los Bukis' romantic-grupera blueprint, has a Tour Gratitud 2026 routing through North America this fall, per 313 Presents. Expect the numbers to recede until next May.
