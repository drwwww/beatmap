Bon Jovi — Livin' On A Prayer

Sports broadcasts remain the surest route back onto the charts for this 1986 arena staple, which is broadly present across 103 country and platform charts today after a stadium airing sent it surging. uDiscover Music documented a 1,325% streaming spike during Japan's World Cup matches, when the track played at full volume as players returned to the pitch after halftime — audible even to television viewers through the stadium mix. The song's football-adjacent afterlife runs deep: TikTok has canonized it as a pre-game walk-up anthem. Its cultural durability is well-earned — the third-ever Bon Jovi track to clear one billion Spotify streams, per Parade. Jon Bon Jovi nearly scrapped it before Richie Sambora and Desmond Child begged him to record it. Its return here follows a 47-day dormancy.
