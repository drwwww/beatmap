A fan moment from Larsson's Midnight Sun Tour touched off a full-scale chart resurgence for her biggest song. During the tour's 2025 run, a fan performed the "Lush Life" choreography onstage with Larsson; the clip went viral and, according to Wikipedia, drove the song back onto charts worldwide. A secondary current has been the "2026 is the new 2016" nostalgia trend circulating online, which has pulled several mid-2010s European pop tracks back into rotation alongside it. NME noted the resurgence as part of a broader Larsson revival anchored by the Midnight Sun campaign — the same cycle that produced her Billboard Global 200-topping "Stateside" remix with PinkPantheress. The electropop track, originally released in 2015 and included on So Good, has now accumulated over 2.23 billion streams on Spotify, per pbmainstream.com. It is currently tracked across 99 country and platform charts.
