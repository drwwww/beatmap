Nelly, Kelly Rowland — Dilemma (feat. Kelly Rowland)

A summer staple of early-2000s radio is broadly charting again, appearing across 14 country and platform charts after a three-week absence. Built on a sample of Patti LaBelle's "Love, Need and Want You," the track paired Nelly's St. Louis drawl with Kelly Rowland's first major outing beyond Destiny's Child, then on hiatus. It topped the Billboard Hot 100 and won the pair a Grammy, as Wikipedia notes, becoming the cornerstone of Nelly's chart-dominating Nellyville run alongside "Hot in Herre." The song endures as the definitive crossover duet of the era — hip-hop verse, pop hook, and a chorus engineered to outlast its decade. Its return today extends a familiar pattern for the cut, which resurfaces in cycles rather than fading.
