A live set at Les Déferlantes has pushed one of Aya Nakamura's biggest hits back into broad rotation. Closing out the festival's second night at Le Barcarès on July 13, the French-Malian star ended her show on a run of Copines, Djadja and Pookie, per L'Indépendant, sending the crowd into its loudest reaction of the evening. The song — a slinky Afropop-inflected cut built on Nakamura's clipped, slang-heavy delivery — has re-entered after a 55-day absence and is now tracked across 31 country and platform charts. Its live pull is nothing new: Copines opened Nakamura's set at the Paris Olympics ceremony, as Paris Match reported. Festival season is doing the rest.
