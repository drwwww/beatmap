Boris Brejcha — Flying Bird

2008 minimal technotechnomelodic techno 🇩🇪 DE

Trending since June 28, 2026 · last seen July 21, 2026 · peaked at 201 charts

An 18-year-old cut from German producer Boris Brejcha's album Mein Wahres Ich is broadly charting today, tracked across 137 country and platform charts and running well above its recent baseline. Brejcha is the architect of high-tech minimal — a precise, clockwork strain of techno defined by intricate percussion and long, patient builds — and Flying Bird is one of its earlier statements, a nearly-eight-minute piece that unfolds rather than drops. He has since become one of the most-booked names in electronic music, known for performing in a Venetian Joker mask, which has kept his older catalog in circulation as new listeners work backward through it. The track sits among his most durable deep cuts, the kind of extended, hypnotic build that rewards the full runtime.
