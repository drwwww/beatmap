Bad Bunny — Yonaguni

2021 reggaetontrap latinolatin 🇵🇷 PR

Trending since June 12, 2026 · last seen June 23, 2026 · peaked at 138 charts

A five-year-old summer ballad from the King of Latin Trap is broadly charting across 138 country and platform charts today, nearly doubling its prior week's footprint. The likeliest driver is renewed attention to Bad Bunny following his Super Bowl LX halftime show, a set that, per Wikipedia, divided the stage into vignettes built to celebrate "real people in everyday life" within the Latin community. Released through Rimas Entertainment in June 2021, Yonaguni was his fourth Billboard Hot 100 top 10 and his first as a solo credit with no featured acts. Named after a Japanese island, it pairs reggaeton beats with a Japanese-language outro — what NPR called a "downbeat, melancholic summer anthem." Its return tracks the seasonal pull that made it a hit, amplified by a culture-wide spotlight on its maker.
