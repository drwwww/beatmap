Imagine Dragons — Bad Liar

2018 🇺🇸 US

Trending since June 26, 2026 · peaked at 122 charts

Oh, hush, my dear, it's been a difficult year
And terrors don't prey on innocent victims
Trust me, darlin', trust me darlin'

Back across the global charts after a 27-day absence, Bad Liar is broadly present today, tracked on 122 country and platform charts. The fourth single from Origins, Imagine Dragons' 2018 follow-up to Evolve, the track is the band's most restrained moment — a piano-led confessional built around Dan Reynolds' fractured falsetto rather than the arena-stomp choruses of Radioactive or Thunder. Written by all four members, it traded the band's maximalist rock signatures for something closer to wounded pop balladry, and that pivot is part of why it has aged into a streaming staple. The Las Vegas group has shifted 111.5 million certified units per the RIAA, but Bad Liar remains the quiet outlier in a catalog defined by volume. Its current spread across charts continues a pattern of periodic resurgence.
