Enya — Only Time

2000 new ageceltic 🇮🇪 IE

Trending since July 30, 2026 · peaked at 71 charts

Who can say where the road goes
Where the day flows, Only time
And who can say if your love grows
As your heart chose, Only time

A quarter-century after its release, the Irish singer's most durable single is broadly charting today, tracked across 71 country and platform charts after an 18-day absence. The song's afterlife has always run on placements: it soundtracked the Friends season-eight episode "The One Where Chandler Takes A Bath," per Songfacts, and returned to the Hot 100 in late 2013 on the back of Volvo Trucks' Van Damme "Epic Split" campaign, one of the decade's most-viewed ad spots. It also became an anthem for September 11th victims, prompting a charity re-release whose proceeds went to first responders. The layered vocals and glacial arrangement made it a fixture of the early-2000s new-age crossover — the rare top-10 US hit for an artist who releases sparingly. Its broad presence today extends that unusually long tail.
