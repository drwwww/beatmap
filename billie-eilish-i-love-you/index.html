A hushed deep cut from Eilish's 2019 debut is back on 129 country and platform charts after a 53-day absence, its whispered dynamics finding a fresh audience through television. The likeliest engine is a reality-TV placement: a Love Island US scene set to the track has circulated on TikTok, with viewers singling out the song as the moment's defining detail. The ballad has a track record as a scene-setter — Netflix notes it soundtracks an intimate first moment in an Italian series' Season 2 opener. Buried thirteenth on When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go?, it's among the most restrained things Eilish and Finneas O'Connell have released — a quiet duet built almost entirely on held breath and a guitar figure. Reality-TV sync remains the reliable resurfacing mechanism for her catalog's slow-burners.
