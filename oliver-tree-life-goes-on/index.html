The song is back across 141 country and platform charts after a five-week absence, and the reason is grim: Oliver Tree, the eccentric Santa Cruz singer-songwriter behind viral hits like "Alien Boy" and "Miss You," has died at 32 in a helicopter crash in Brazil, Rolling Stone reports. The track — released through Atlantic in 2021 as a deluxe cut from Ugly Is Beautiful, produced with Getter — became his signature, and its chorus is now soundtracking memorial edits and tribute clips across TikTok. A resurfaced clip in which he discussed the helicopter weeks before the crash has compounded the grief-driven spike. Catalog resurgences of this scale following an artist's death tend to hold for weeks; the mordant title has become its own eulogy.
