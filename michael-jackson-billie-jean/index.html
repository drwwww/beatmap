The Lionsgate biopic Michael has reanimated Jackson's catalog, and its second single is leading the charge — appearing on 169 country and platform charts today and returning to number one on the Global Spotify chart with 6.23 million streams, per YouTube. The teaser trailer, released November 6, 2025, threads the Billie Jean groove through a mashup of "Wanna Be Startin' Somethin'," "Beat It" and "Human Nature," according to Wikipedia. Streaming bore it out: Chartmetric clocked the song climbing from 53.9 million to 130.4 million plays post-release. NPR notes the film drew near-universal critical panning while the renewed interest in MJ's discography proved just as universal. By Billboard's count, the 1982 single again briefly stood as the biggest song in the world.
