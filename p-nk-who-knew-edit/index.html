"Who Knew" is back across 107 country and platform charts, returning after a brief gap in broad presence. The track is the emotional centerpiece of I'm Not Dead, P!nk's 2006 album — she has described it as being about the death of friendship, written specifically in response to friends she lost to drug overdoses. That weight sits underneath what reads on the surface as a breakup anthem, which is partly why the song has never fully settled into nostalgia: it carries a grief that doesn't date. The edit trims the album version without losing the chorus's blunt, compressed force — the kind of arrangement that travels well across streaming playlists built around mood rather than era. P!nk remains a consistent stadium-circuit presence, and catalog tracks from I'm Not Dead tend to resurface whenever her touring profile is high.
