A Grammy-winning cut from DAMN. is broadly charting today, tracked across 144 country and platform charts. Built on a chopped, backwards-sampling groove and a rare full-verse Rihanna feature, the track has always been one of the more elastic songs in Kendrick Lamar's catalog — hook-forward enough for radio, dense enough for the Dissect podcast to devote an entire episode to unpacking its king-and-savage self-mythology. The pairing remains a commercial anomaly: two artists at the peak of their respective genres, sharing three-and-a-half minutes that neither has revisited on record since. Nearly a decade past its release, the song still sits among the most streamed entries from DAMN., and its presence across so many charts at once marks a genuine current moment worth noting.
