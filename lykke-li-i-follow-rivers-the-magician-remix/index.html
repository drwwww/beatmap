The song that turned Lykke Li's brooding folk single into a billion-stream dance record is broadly charting again, appearing across 112 country and platform charts today after a two-month absence. The trigger is a full-circle reunion: The Magician, real name Stephen Fasano, marked the remix's 15th anniversary in June by reconnecting with the Swedish artist for the first time, according to We Rave You, and the pair have since released a new rework of her track "Lucky Again," per Backseat Mafia. The renewed press cycle has driven listeners back to the original. Its cinematic afterlife runs deep — the remix soundtracked Jacques Audiard's Rust and Bone and Palme d'Or winner Blue Is the Warmest Colour, notes Wikipedia. Anniversary-driven resurgences like this tend to spike sharply, then settle.
