Música Nocheto — Duérmete Niño

(Sleep, Child) 2018

Trending since May 27, 2026 · peaked at 104 charts

Spanish-language children's lullabies occupy a reliable corner of the global streaming charts, and Duérmete Niño is among the steadiest performers in that space — currently tracked across 104 country and platform charts after returning from a brief two-week gap. Música Nocheto is a Spanish-language children's music project whose catalog centers on canciones de cuna — traditional cradle songs arranged for streaming-era nursery playlists — and this track sits near the top of that catalog. Its draw is functional: soft, repetitive, unhurried, the kind of recording that parents queue on loop at 2 a.m. and forget to turn off. No specific sync or cultural event appears to be driving the current run.
