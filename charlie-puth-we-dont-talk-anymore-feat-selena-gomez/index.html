The Charlie Puth–Selena Gomez duet is back across the global charts today, tracked on 87 country and platform charts after a 69-day absence. Originally the third single from Puth's 2016 debut Nine Track Mind, the song reached number nine on the Billboard Hot 100, becoming his second top-ten hit and Gomez's sixth, per Wikipedia. Its durability rests on a clean call-and-response structure — two voices circling the same phrase — that reads as a breakup post-mortem and translates easily to short-form clips. The pairing carried its own subplot: Gomez and Puth sparked dating rumors when they performed the duet live on her Revival tour, according to AOL. A steady catalog performer, it re-enters at broad scale.
