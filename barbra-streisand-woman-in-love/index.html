Barbra Streisand — Woman in Love

A 45-year-old ballad written by the Bee Gees is back on the global charts, appearing across 84 country and platform charts today after a two-month absence. Written by Barry and Robin Gibb for Streisand's 1980 album Guilty, it earned the brothers a 1980 Ivor Novello award for best song and became the fourth of Streisand's four Platinum singles, per Wikipedia. The Gibbs' fingerprints are all over it — the soaring melodic arcs and layered harmonies are pure late-period Bee Gees, filtered through one of the most controlled voices in American pop. Its music video famously stitched together scenes from Streisand's 1970s films, including A Star Is Born. The track has crossed a quarter-billion Spotify streams and remains one of her most durable recordings.
