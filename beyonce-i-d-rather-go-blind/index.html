Beyoncé — I'd Rather Go Blind

2008 🇺🇸 US

Beyoncé's cover of the Etta James standard is broadly charting today, tracking across 138 country and platform charts. The song originated as a performance piece: recorded for the Cadillac Records soundtrack, it captured Beyoncé inhabiting James onscreen in the 2008 biopic, and the resulting vocal — rawer and more unguarded than most of her studio work — has since developed a durable second life independent of the film. Per Tunefind, it most recently surfaced on American Idol Season 15, Episode 3, a placement that reflects how deeply the track has entered the audition-repertoire canon for singers demonstrating range and emotional control. The original 1968 recording has been covered by everyone from Christine Perfect (later Christine McVie of Fleetwood Mac) to Rod Stewart, but this version occupies its own lane — a film-performance recording that functions more like a live document than a studio single.
